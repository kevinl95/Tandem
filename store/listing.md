## Long description

Limit: 4,000 characters. Shown on the Appstore website and tablets, not on Fire TV.

> Tandem Screen Share puts your computer, Android phone or Fire tablet screen on your Fire TV.
>
> Open the app on your Fire TV, then go to tandemscreen.com on a computer on the same Wi-Fi. Your TV appears in the list. Pick it, choose what to share, and press Allow on the TV the first time. That's it: no account, no sign-in and no cables.
>
> Your screen and sound travel directly from your device to your TV over your home network, encrypted, and never pass through our servers. A small service only helps the two devices find each other.
>
> What you can share:
> - A whole screen, a window or a single browser tab from Chrome, Edge or Firefox on Windows, Mac, Linux or Chromebook. Sound works when you share a tab in Chrome or Edge.
> - An Android phone or tablet, including Fire tablets from Fire OS 5 onward, with the free Android app from tandemscreen.com. Sound is included on Android 10 and later.
>
> You stay in control. Nothing appears on your TV until someone presses Allow with the remote. Devices you allow are remembered, and you can block a device or forget all of them from Allowed devices on the TV. Press Back to stop a share at any time.
>
> Good to know: both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks and some office networks stop devices from talking to each other, and a VPN on the sending device can block the connection. iPhone and iPad aren't supported yet.
>
> Privacy policy: https://tandemscreen.com/privacy.html

## Testing instructions for Amazon's reviewers

> Tandem Screen Share needs a second device on the same Wi-Fi network as the Fire TV: a computer with Chrome, Edge or Firefox, or an Android device with our app. Use a network that lets devices reach each other (not a guest network with client isolation), and turn off any VPN on the sending device. The Fire TV needs internet access so the two devices can find each other; the video itself stays on the local network.
>
> 1. Launch Tandem Screen Share on the Fire TV. It shows "Tandem Screen Share", a six-character code, and "On a computer on this WiFi, go to tandemscreen.com".
> 2. On the computer, open https://tandemscreen.com. Within about 15 seconds the TV appears under "TVs on your network" as "Fire TV" followed by its code. If it doesn't (for example, if the computer uses a different internet connection), type the code from the TV into "Or enter the code shown on the TV" and click Share.
> 3. Click the TV and choose a screen, window or tab in the browser's prompt. For sound, choose a Chrome or Edge tab and tick "Also share tab audio".
> 4. The TV asks "<device name> wants to share its screen". Press Select on the remote to choose Allow.
> 5. The shared screen appears full screen on the TV. To stop, press Back on the remote (the TV returns to its code) or click "Stop sharing" on the computer.
>
> Other things to try:
> - Share again from the same computer: it connects without asking, because the TV remembers allowed devices.
> - On the pairing screen, press Right to reach "Allowed devices". Use Up/Down and Select to Forget or Unblock a device, and Back to close.
> - When the TV asks for approval, choose Block. Further shares from that device are declined without a prompt.
> - Press Home during a share: sharing and sound stop. Reopen the app and it reconnects with the same code.
> - After 15 minutes on the pairing screen with no share, the TV pauses its connection to save resources. Press Select (OK) to resume.
> - Android: install the app from https://tandemscreen.com/downloads/tandem.apk (Android 5.1 or later), open it, tap the TV and allow screen capture.
>
> Contact: support@tandemscreen.com
